Privacy Policy

Gulf Limousine Travel · Cairo, Egypt · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what the Gulf Limousine Travel app (Android, iOS and web) collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Name, email, phone numberYour account, booking confirmations and contacting you about a rental.
National ID number, photos of your ID and driving licenceRequired to verify the driver before handing over a car.
Profile photo (optional)Shown in your profile.
Bookings: car, dates, pickup and drop-off addressesDelivering and collecting the car, and billing.
Car condition photos and videos, odometer photosRecording the car's condition and kilometres at pickup and return.
Payment records: amount, method, transaction ID, transfer receiptsConfirming payments and keeping invoices. We never see or store card numbers.
Your location, only while you use the mapCentring the map and filling in an address. It is not tracked in the background.
Messages to the in-app assistantAnswering your questions.
Device notification tokenSending booking and payment notifications.

Fingerprint or Face ID login is handled by your phone. Your biometric data never leaves the device; the app only stores, in the phone's secure storage, the sign-in it unlocks.

The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database and notifications.
  • Cloudinary: storage of uploaded photos and videos.
  • Paymob: card and mobile-wallet payments. Card details are entered on Paymob's page, not in the app.
  • Google Gemini: answers messages sent to the in-app assistant.
  • Vercel: hosts the small server that creates bookings and payments.
  • OpenStreetMap: map images.

Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

Your profile is kept until you delete your account. Booking, payment and traffic-ticket records are kept after deletion because Egyptian law requires us to keep invoices and accounting records.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile (including your ID and licence details), favorites, notifications, assistant chat history, the saved fingerprint login on that device and your sign-in. Uploaded photos are no longer linked to you; email us if you also want the stored files erased. You can also ask us by email to delete the whole account.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in Profile at any time, and ask us for a copy of your data or its deletion.

Children

The app is for adults who can legally rent or book a car. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
